AwardDefinition

🧊 Biggest Dud The starter who missed his projection by the most, among those projected for 10 or more.
🍀 Luckiest Bastard Won the week with the score least likely to have won it.
😭 Most Robbed Lost the week with the score most likely to have won it.

🤡 Clown Move The single benched-for-started swap that cost the most points.
