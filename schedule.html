2026 schedule

84 games over 14 weeks. Who plays who, and why.

How it works

  • There are 12 managers. Everyone plays everyone else once. That fills 11 weeks.
  • The season is 14 weeks long, so 3 weeks are left over.
  • Those weeks are rematches. 18 pairs play twice, and every manager gets exactly 3 rematches.
  • Nobody plays the same person three times. There is no room for it.

So the only real choice is who gets the rematches. They go to the managers who have played each other the least. This year those pairs have met 3.9 times on average. Across the whole league the average is 5.2.
