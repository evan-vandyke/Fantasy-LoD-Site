Escaped the trash 21 Aug 2026

Under Moves > Activity, it shows I have zero trades but Adam and I completed a trade last season. Do trades show for everyone involved or just the person that initiated the trade.

— Greg

Trades count for both managers, not just the one who started it. ESPN records a trade once for each side, and the record for Greg's side arrived with no players in it. The site only counted a trade when it could see players move, so it missed this one. Fixed, and it turned up two more: ESPN had also dropped Adam's side of this trade and of a 2022 one with Stew, which we put back by hand. Every trade count on the site now matches ESPN, season by season.
